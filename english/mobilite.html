International Mobility

As part of my Digital Sciences engineering degree at ENSEEIHT, and in line with my apprenticeship at Orange Business (Network, Cloud, and Security Solutions Engineer), I plan to complete an exchange semester abroad to further specialize in cybersecurity and cloud computing.

This international experience will notably allow me to:

  • deepen my technical skills in secure, cloud-native architectures;
  • discover new teaching methodologies focused on hands-on projects and applied research;
  • thrive in an international and multicultural environment;
  • strengthen my adaptability to European digital challenges.

Why Switzerland and Austria?

A Demanding Technological Ecosystem

Switzerland and Austria boast cutting-edge digital infrastructures, a dense industrial network, and a strong culture of information systems security and data protection.

Continuity with My Apprenticeship

  • Alignment with European standards: regulations, digital sovereignty, and critical architectures.
  • English-taught programs: international curricula allowing me to earn ECTS credits while strengthening my technical English.
  • Applied approach: strong connections between university laboratories and real-world industry needs.

FHNW — Switzerland

Why This University?

The Fachhochschule Nordwestschweiz (FHNW) is recognized for its practice-oriented teaching and close ties with the Swiss business ecosystem (Basel / Brugg-Windisch region). It emphasizes solving real-world engineering problems in laboratory settings.

Target Courses (Cyber & Cloud)

  • Cloud Computing & Distributed Architectures: virtualization, containerization, and cloud infrastructure deployment.
  • Network and System Security: infrastructure hardening, intrusion detection, and network defense.
  • Applied Computer Engineering Projects: team-based implementation of secure solutions.

BFH — Switzerland

Why This University?

The Berner Fachhochschule (Bern University of Applied Sciences) enjoys an outstanding reputation in applied computer science and digital security, right at the heart of the Swiss federal capital where reliability and confidentiality standards are exceptionally high.

Target Courses (Cyber & Cloud)

  • Information & Cyber Security: vulnerability analysis, penetration testing, and risk management.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & DevSecOps: automation, cloud platform administration, and security by design.
  • Applied Cryptography: securing communications and Identity & Access Management (IAM).

AAU Klagenfurt — Austria

Why This University?

The Alpen-Adria-Universität Klagenfurt stands out for the quality of its computer science and networking programs, as well as its close integration with the Lakeside Science & Technology Park, an innovation hub bringing together tech companies and R&D centers.

Target Courses (Cyber & Cloud)

  • System & Network Security: protocol security, infrastructure protection, and threat analysis.
  • Cloud & Distributed Systems: designing resilient distributed services and scalable architectures.
  • Privacy & Data Protection: technical and regulatory challenges of data protection in cloud environments.
